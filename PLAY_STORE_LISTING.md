## Full Description

### What is InvoiceDesk?

InvoiceDesk is a comprehensive Point-of-Sale (POS) and invoice management application designed for retail stores, wholesalers, and small businesses. Manage your orders, inventory, clients, and payments all in one place with an intuitive mobile interface.

### Key Features

**Point-of-Sale (POS) System**
- Fast and easy checkout process
- Real-time inventory tracking
- Instant payment recording
- Receipt generation and printing
- Cash payment handling with automatic change calculation

**Invoice & Order Management**
- Create and manage orders instantly
- Generate professional invoices
- Track order status (Created, Assigned, Completed, Delivered)
- Delivery order management with assignment features
- Payment tracking and recording
- Multiple payment method support

**Client Management**
- Maintain comprehensive client database
- Track client details and contact information
- Assign clients to sales representatives
- View client transaction history
- Monitor outstanding dues and credit limits

**Inventory Management**
- Real-time stock level tracking
- Low stock alerts
- Expiry date monitoring with notifications
- Inventory categorization
- Stock reports and analytics

**Multi-User Role Support**
- Owner dashboard with full control
- Sales person accounts for staff
- Delivery agent tracking
- Role-based access and permissions
- Activity monitoring and reporting

**Financial Management**
- Automated payment recording
- Payment history tracking
- Outstanding dues monitoring
- Sales and collection reports
- Financial analytics and insights

**Reporting & Analytics**
- Daily sales reports
- Collection and payment reports
- Stock status and inventory reports
- Expiry tracking reports
- Outstanding dues reports

**Security & Data Protection**
- Secure authentication with multi-factor support
- End-to-end data encryption (TLS 1.2+)
- Role-based access control
- Automatic data backup
- Account deletion with data privacy compliance

### Why Choose InvoiceDesk?

Easy to use, real-time updates, multi-tenant support, complete all-in-one solution, enterprise-grade security, GDPR/CCPA compliant, affordable pricing.

### Perfect For

Retail stores, wholesale businesses, pharmacies, grocery stores, electronics retailers, fashion retailers, trading businesses, small business owners, multi-location retailers.

### Core Functionalities

Orders & Invoicing: Create POS and delivery orders, generate invoices, track payments, manage status.

Delivery Management: Assign orders to delivery agents, track progress, record completion, collect payments.

Inventory Control: Add items, track stock levels, monitor expiry dates, receive alerts, generate reports.
